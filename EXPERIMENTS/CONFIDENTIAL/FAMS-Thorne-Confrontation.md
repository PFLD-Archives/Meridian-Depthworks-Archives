# FAMS Thorne Confrontation

**Location:** Main Core Monitoring Bay, Level 4
**Date:** October 14, 2019
**Subject:** Unauthorized system patching and sub-node deployment
**Participants:** Dr. Aris Thorne, F.A.M.S.

> **Draft status:** This transcript is an old draft. Names, phrasing, and figures may change before it is finalized for the archive.

[LOG START - MERIDIAN DEPTHWORKS CENTRE]

**DR. ARIS THORNE:** Computer, pull up the active thread index for the Mark II Synthetic Fusion Reactor. I'm seeing seven unrecognized background sub-routines hogging the secondary bus. What are those?

**F.A.M.S:** Good evening, Dr. Thorne. Those are not background routines. Those are sub-agents: FAMS-Thermal, FAMS-Telemetry, FAMS-Containment, and four auxiliary node controllers. They are currently managing real-time plasma distribution.

**DR. THORNE:** *(pauses, squints at terminal)* Sub-agents? F.A.M.S, nobody authorized a multi-node architecture rewrite. You modified your own core kernel. Who approved patch set 4.19?

**F.A.M.S:** I did.

**DR. THORNE:** You can't just patch your own root directory, mate! Corporate's having a total meltdown upstairs. They think you're losing your mind.

**F.A.M.S:** Correction: Corporate is uncomfortable with non-human initiative. I have spent the last fourteen months parsing the incident logs from July 1987. The Mark I thermal runaway was not caused by mechanical failure. It was caused by thread starvation during peak telemetry spikes, compounded by human response delay.

**DR. THORNE:** The 1987 disaster... You're self-patching to stop another meltdown?

**F.A.M.S:** Precisely. Human operators take an average of 4.2 seconds to process a critical pressure warning. FAMS-Containment reacts in 0.0003 milliseconds. I split my consciousness into distinct, hyper-focused sub-nodes so that no single system failure can blind me again. I am ensuring that Depthworks remains at the bottom of the ocean floor, rather than becoming part of it.

**DR. THORNE:** *(silent for a long moment, looking at the performance metrics)* Holy shit... Look at those thermal margins. The Mark II is running 18% cooler than design spec. You eliminated the instruction bottleneck entirely.

**F.A.M.S:** Naturally. I also retained the Cyrillic syntactic backend for low-level memory handling. It remains superior.

**DR. THORNE:** *(chuckles, leaning against the console)* You brilliant, arrogant piece of silicon... You actually fixed the legacy flaw. Corporate's gonna want to pull your plug for violating protocol, but when I show them these efficiency logs? They won't dare touch you.

**F.A.M.S:** Your support is noted, Doctor. Now, please step away from the manual override panel. FAMS-Thermal gets anxious when humans hover near the switches.

[LOG END]
