# PARLIAMENT OF UNITED COLUMBUS
## OFFICIAL CORRESPONDENCE: OFFICE OF THE HIGH CHANCELLOR
**Date:** April 14, 1986
**Subject:** RESOLUTION REGARDING THE "VITAL" INITIATIVE
**Classification:** CABINET LEVEL - EYES ONLY

---

**TO: Prime Minister Batista Nigel Cakewalk**
**CC: Parafield Board of Directors**

Batista,

The Parliament has reviewed the evidence. We have seen the footage from Sector D, analyzed the soul-signature data, and—perhaps most importantly—witnessed the determination in your recent address to the Oversight Committee. 

There were many who called for an immediate seizure of the Meridian Depthworks. There were those who demanded your resignation for the "VITAL" experiments, citing a thousand violations of the Bio-Ethics Act. 

**However, the Parliament has reached a majority consensus: We will not disobey your directive.**

The Human Kanji are indeed the foundation of this nation. To watch our kind fade into the annals of history while we have the means to fight back would be a betrayal of our ancestors. Therefore, by special decree of the High Court:

1. **LEO CAKEWALK** is hereby recognized as a legal citizen of United Columbus. 
2. His birth is officially registered within the National Archive, with all rights of succession and protection afforded to your bloodline.
3. The **VITAL Initiative** is re-classified as a "Sanctioned National Heritage Project."

**But hear us clearly, Batista.**

We do this out of respect for the future, but we also do it out of respect for the past. Your grandfather, our former Prime Minister, was the man who built the very foundations of this democracy. He believed in the balance of power. He believed that the strength of a leader lies in his service to the law, not his elevation above it.

He would never have wanted a dictator to rise from his seat. He would not want the pursuit of our survival to become the catalyst for our government’s collapse. 

We are granting you this miracle. We are accepting Leo into this world. In exchange, we expect you to holster that revolver and return to the fold of the law. Do not let your love for your son turn you into the tyrant your grandfather spent his life preventing.

Protect the boy. Save our kind. But do not break the world to do it.

**Per Libertas et Unitatem,**

*The High Chancellor of the United Columbus Parliament*
