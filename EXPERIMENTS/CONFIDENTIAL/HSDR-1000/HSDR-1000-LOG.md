**Author:** Pablo Cakewalk

**Location:** Private Workshop, Undisclosed

**Security Clearance:** CONFIDENTIAL (Family Access Only, apparently that means nothing to me)

## I. WHY NOT?
Everyone at Parafield keeps telling me the S.F.R. is too big, too dangerous, too "not something you touch without six years of reactor operations training." Fine. I'm not touching the S.F.R. I'm making my own.

If Batista gets to have a superstructure that punches out 80% of the country's power, I get to have something that fits on my back and punches a hole through a wall. Fair trade.

## II. THE FUEL PROBLEM
Obviously I needed an isotope. Reactive was tempting, more output, more fun, but even I know a 50% reduced symmetry margin means one bad step and I'm not writing any more of these logs. Efficient lasts longer but barely gets hot enough to be worth strapping to my spine.

Generic is the move. Balanced burn rate, predictable below 35 keV, and I get a full day of charge before the cell's dead. I'm not trying to hit Shatter Point on purpose. I just want a laser that could ruin someone's day from thirty feet away.

## III. THE BACKPACK RIG
Obviously I can't fit six C-2500s on my back. So I didn't try.

* **Single Emitter Port:** Instead of six lasers converging on a pellet in perfect symmetry, I built one high-output discharge chamber with an internal focusing lens. No convergent compression, no true ignition, just a directed channel for the isotope's neutron-pump reaction to escape through.
* **The Tradeoff:** It's not clean fusion. It's not going to power a city. But it doesn't need six-way symmetry to be dangerous, it just needs to go in a straight line.
* **Containment:** Borrowed some baffle logic from the S.F.R. spec, scaled down obviously. Enough shielding that I don't cook myself holding it, not enough that this thing is safe by any actual Parafield standard.

## IV. FIRST LIVE TEST
Fired it at a slab of reinforced concrete out back. It's gone. Not "damaged," gone. There's a hole clean through the other side and the edges are still glowing.

My eyebrows are also gone. Worth it.

## V. WHAT I'M CALLING IT
HSDR-1000. Handheld Synthetic Death Ray. I know it's on the nose. I don't care, it's accurate.

I need to show Batista before he hears about the concrete slab from someone else.
