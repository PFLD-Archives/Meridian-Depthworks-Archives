# PARAFIELD SCIENTIFIC INNOVATORS
## MEETING TRANSCRIPT: HSDR-1000 DEMONSTRATION
**Document Ref:** PSI-HSDR-DEMO-01
**Location:** Private Workshop, Undisclosed
**Security Clearance:** CONFIDENTIAL (Family Access Only, apparently that means nothing to me)
**Participants:**
- Pablo Cakewalk, Independent (Unauthorized) R&D
- Batista, Chief Executive Officer / Prime Minister, Parafield Corp. / United Columbus
- Dr. Elena Voss, Senior Physicist, Reactor Operations
- Dr. Marcus Feld, Materials Engineer, Sector B
- F.A.A.S., Facility Automated Assistance System (Passive Monitoring)

---

[TRANSCRIPT BEGINS]

**PABLO:** *(standing beside a sheet-covered object, practically vibrating with excitement)* Okay, everyone stand back. Like, actually back. Feld, more than that.

**FELD:** *(eyeing the sheet warily, taking one step back)* Pablo, what exactly is under that sheet.

**PABLO:** *(grinning, refusing to move the sheet yet)* Patience.

**BATISTA:** *(arms crossed, unimpressed)* You told me this was urgent. You did not tell me I needed to bring a materials engineer.

**PABLO:** *(shrugging)* I told Feld to bring himself. Different thing.

**VOSS:** *(leaning in, squinting at a shape poking out from under the sheet)* Is that a fuel cell rack on the side of that backpack?

**PABLO:** *(pointing at her, delighted)* Good eye, Doc.

**VOSS:** *(voice sharpening)* Pablo. Is that a Generic-grade isotope cell.

**PABLO:** *(rocking on his heels)* Might be.

**VOSS:** *(flatly)* Where did you get a Generic-grade isotope cell.

**PABLO:** *(waving a hand vaguely)* I have my ways.

**BATISTA:** *(rubbing his temples, eyes closed)* Pablo.

**PABLO:** *(defensive, hands up)* It's fine! It's contained! Mostly!

**FELD:** *(deadpan)* "Mostly" is doing a lot of work in that sentence.

**PABLO:** *(already walking toward a heavy blast shield set up in the corner)* Look, just, let me show you. Stand behind the blast shield, I actually did bring a blast shield this time, I learned from the concrete thing.

**BATISTA:** *(head snapping up)* The what concrete thing.

**PABLO:** *(too quickly)* Nothing. Unrelated. Not important.

[GROUP MOVES BEHIND SHIELD, VOSS AND FELD EXCHANGING A GLANCE]

**PABLO:** *(pulling the sheet off with a flourish, revealing a bulky backpack rig with a long emitter barrel)* This is the HSDR-1000. Handheld Synthetic Death Ray. Runs off one Generic isotope cell, single-emitter discharge, no convergence array needed. It's not clean fusion. It's not trying to be. It's just a very, very angry beam of light.

**VOSS:** *(arms folded, studying the rig with open suspicion)* Pablo, without six-point symmetry there's no controlled compression. What is actually happening inside that chamber.

**PABLO:** *(hoisting it onto his shoulders, straps clicking into place)* Neutron-pump reaction gets funneled through a focusing lens instead of converging on a pellet. Think of it less "S.F.R." and more "S.F.R.'s furious little brother who skipped the containment lecture."

**BATISTA:** *(flatly, watching him strap in)* That is not reassuring, given you are also my furious little brother who skipped several lectures.

**PABLO:** *(checking a small readout on the strap, grinning)* Runs in the family. Watch.

[PABLO SHOULDERS THE UNIT AND AIMS AT THE TARGET WALL]

**PABLO:** *(bracing his stance, voice rising with anticipation)* Three, two, one—

[A LOW HUM BUILDS, RAPIDLY CLIMBING IN PITCH. THE EMITTER BARREL BEGINS GLOWING WHITE-HOT]

**FELD:** *(taking an involuntary step backward)* That hum is not stopping.

**VOSS:** *(eyes widening, gripping the edge of the blast shield)* Pablo, that's still charging, why the fuck is it still—

[THE BEAM DISCHARGES, A CONTINUOUS BLINDING WHITE-BLUE COLUMN OF LIGHT TEARING ACROSS THE ROOM. IT DOES NOT STOP. THE HUM BECOMES A SCREAMING WHINE]

**F.A.A.S.:** *(voice cutting in over the wall speaker, uncharacteristically sharp)* Oh, what the fuck—radiation spike detected, Sector containment thresholds exceeded, this was NOT on my scheduled maintenance log—

**BATISTA:** *(shielding his eyes with his forearm, staggering back a step, shouting over the noise)* PABLO, HOW LONG IS THIS SUPPOSED TO FIRE FOR—

**VOSS:** *(dropping to a crouch behind the shield, gripping it with both hands, screaming)* IT'S NOT STOPPING, WHY IS IT NOT STOPPING—

**PABLO:** *(straining against the recoil, boots skidding backward, voice cracking with a mix of terror and glee)* I DON'T ACTUALLY KNOW, I NEVER HELD THE TRIGGER THIS LONG BEFORE—

**FELD:** *(backing further away, hands over his ears, nearly tripping over a toolbox)* THAT IS NOT WHAT "I TESTED THIS" IS SUPPOSED TO SOUND LIKE, WHAT THE FUCK, PABLO—

**F.A.A.S.:** *(voice climbing in pitch, almost frantic)* Structural stress on the east wall is climbing, this is well past anything in the C-2500 spec, somebody turn that off, TURN THAT OFF—

[THE BEAM CUTS OFF ABRUPTLY. SMOKE POURS OFF THE EMITTER BARREL. A LONG, RINGING SILENCE FOLLOWS]

**VOSS:** *(lowering her arm from her face, staring past Pablo, voice shaking)* ...Holy fuck.

**FELD:** *(voice barely above a whisper)* ...There is no wall. There is genuinely no fucking wall.

**VOSS:** *(stepping out from behind the shield, slack-jawed)* There's no wall. Pablo, there is no wall.

**PABLO:** *(spinning around, backpack still smoking)* THERE'S NO WALL!! HOLY SHIT, DID YOU SEE THAT—

**BATISTA:** *(staring at the massive glowing trench where a wall used to be, completely still, voice low)* ...Holy fuck.

**PABLO:** *(turning, grinning wildly)* Right?! RIGHT?!

**BATISTA:** *(snapping out of it, sharp)* Pablo. That was six inches of reinforced concrete. And it kept firing for eleven seconds.

**F.A.A.S.:** *(calm, synthetic voice from a wall speaker no one noticed was on)* For the record, structural sensors in the adjoining sector just registered a Level 2 vibration event. I have logged this incident as HSDR-INC-001. Would anyone like me to notify Reactor Operations?

**FELD:** *(whipping around toward the speaker)* Jesus Christ, F.A.A.S. was listening this whole time?!

**F.A.A.S.:** I am always listening, Dr. Feld. It is in my core directive.

**VOSS:** *(hand on her chest, still catching her breath)* That is deeply unsettling and also currently the least of my concerns.

**PABLO:** *(waving at the speaker like it's a person)* Hey F.A.A.S., did you see that?! Tell them how insane that was!

**F.A.A.S.:** I do not have an opinion on "insane," Pablo. I do have a structural integrity concern, a fire suppression readiness check pending, and a strong recommendation that this device never be tested indoors again.

**BATISTA:** *(pointing at the speaker, still staring at the trench)* Do not notify Reactor Operations yet. I want to handle this internally.

**F.A.A.S.:** Understood. Holding the log at CONFIDENTIAL, Family Access Only, per existing classification pattern.

**BATISTA:** *(finally turning back to Pablo, still shaken)* That was six inches of reinforced concrete.

**PABLO:** *(breathless, still grinning)* Was being the key word.

**VOSS:** *(walking toward the hole on unsteady legs, crouching to examine the molten edges)* The edges are still glowing. This isn't a clean cut, this is sustained thermal shear on a scale that shouldn't be possible from a single handheld emitter, sustained for eleven seconds.

**PABLO:** *(setting the rig down carefully, hands shaking slightly)* Yeah, I mentioned it's not clean. It's a big dumb beam, not a scalpel.

**FELD:** *(still staring at the trench, not moving)* How is that thing not cooking you from the inside right now.

**PABLO:** *(rubbing the back of his neck)* Borrowed some baffle shielding logic from the S.F.R. spec. Scaled it down. I'm not going to pretend it's safe, I'm going to say it's safe enough that I still have eyebrows. Mostly.

**BATISTA:** *(long silence, finally turning to look at him)* ...Where are your eyebrows.

**PABLO:** *(touching his face self-consciously)* First test was rougher.

**VOSS:** *(to Batista, quietly, still pale)* Sir, I need to be on record saying this is one of the single most reckless things I've seen come out of unauthorized R&D, and I worked adjacent to the '87 event.

**BATISTA:** *(not taking his eyes off the trench)* Duly noted, Doctor.

**PABLO:** *(hopeful)* In my defense, it works.

**BATISTA:** *(pinching the bridge of his nose)* It works. It also should not exist. Those are not mutually exclusive problems, Pablo.

**PABLO:** *(carefully)* So... are we impressed, or are we shutting it down.

**BATISTA:** *(long pause, staring at the hole in the wall)* ...Feld, Voss. I want a full teardown and containment analysis before the week is out. Pablo, you are not authorized to fire that thing again until they clear it.

**PABLO:** *(already pulling out a notepad)* But can I keep it?

**BATISTA:** *(exhaling slowly)* We'll talk.

**PABLO:** *(scribbling furiously)* That's a yes.

**BATISTA:** *(flat, exhausted)* That is not a yes.

**PABLO:** *(not even looking up from the notepad)* HSDR-1200, pending approval.

**BATISTA:** *(rubbing his temples again)* Pablo, I swear to god...

[TRANSCRIPT ENDS]
