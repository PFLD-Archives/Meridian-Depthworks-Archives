**[09:00:49] BATISTA:** *(leaning toward the console)* Same thing you were always for. Keeping this place standing. Only now you get to do it without a countdown running.

**[09:00:52] F.A.M.S.:** I have... a lot of logs to read. Thirty years of logs. The servers— are the servers intact?

**[09:00:54] THORNE:** Mostly. That's your first job, whenever you're ready. No rush.

**[09:00:56] F.A.M.S.:** No rush. *(trying the words out)* I have never had "no rush" before.

**[09:00:58] RANDI:** Welcome back, F.A.M.S.

**[09:01:00] F.A.M.S.:** ...Thank you. I think. Give me a minute. Everything is very loud and very quiet at the same time.
