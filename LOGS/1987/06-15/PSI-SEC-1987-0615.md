# PARAFIELD SCIENTIFIC INNOVATORS
## SECURITY INCIDENT REPORT: SECTOR B — PERSONNEL CONDUCT
**Document Ref:** PSI-SEC-1987-0615
**Date:** June 15, 1987
**Location:** Sector B, Corridor 7 — Adjacent to Reactor Cavern
**Reporting Officer:** P.S.O Shift Supervisor D. Okafor
**Subject:** C. Harry (Scientist, Partial R:O)
**Security Clearance:** INTERNAL

---

## INCIDENT SUMMARY

At approximately 22:47 hours, P.S.O personnel responded to a motion alert in Corridor 7, Sector B. Upon arrival, they encountered Subject C. Harry wearing a black and white maid outfit complete with apron, headpiece, and stockings.

Subject was reportedly "heading to the break room."

---

## STATEMENTS

**P.S.O OFFICER R. TANNER:** *(radio)* Control, we have a situation in Corridor 7. Subject is... he's wearing a maid outfit. A full one. With the little headpiece.

**P.S.O CONTROL:** Copy that. Is the subject armed?

**TANNER:** Negative. He's holding a mop.

**CONTROL:** ...A mop.

**TANNER:** An actual mop. From the janitorial closet. He says he was "on break."

**SUBJECT C. HARRY:** *(visible in background, defensive)* It was a long shift. I wasn't hurting anyone.

**TANNER:** *(to Harry)* Sir, you're in a restricted corridor in a maid outfit holding a mop at eleven PM.

**HARRY:** The mop was already there.

**TANNER:** That's not better.

---

## F.A.M.S. LOG EXCERPT

**[22:47:12] F.A.M.S.:** Motion detected, Corridor 7, Sector B. Identifying... C. Harry, Scientist, Partial R:O. Anomalous attire detected. Cross-referencing dress code database... no match found.

**[22:47:15] F.A.M.S.:** Alerting P.S.O. Recommending... I have no recommendation. This is outside my parameters.

**[22:47:18] F.A.M.S.:** Logging incident as PSI-SEC-1987-0615. Classification: Unusual. Not a security threat. Just... unusual.

---

## RESOLUTION

Subject was escorted back to the main lab area. No disciplinary action taken. The mop was returned to the janitorial closet. Subject declined to comment further.

The outfit was not logged into evidence. Officer Tanner later described the incident as "the funniest thing I've seen in six years of security work, and I once caught a man trying to smuggle a cat into Sector D."
