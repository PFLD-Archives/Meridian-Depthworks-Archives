# MDC Meltdown News Report

**Date:** 10/18/1987
**Source:** Meridian District news wire
**Subject:** Meridian Depthworks Centre thermal runaway

---

**DATE: 10/18/1987 | MERIDIAN DEPTHWORKS MELTDOWN**

5 days ago, a severe event has occurred at Meridian Depthworks Centre. The S.F.R reactor has entered a thermal runaway around 2:12:02 PM after a failed shutdown attempt was made.

However, thanks to 4 brave personnel. They have successfully stalled the reactor entirely by ejecting 4 of the fuel cells that fueled the S.F.R. They've quickly transported to the hospital however their conditions were been reported to be extremely critical.

As for the centre. Everyone has been evacuated from the facility and sent to a infirmary for immediate check ups. The entire Sector B facility has highly contaminated, Sector C has been reported to have several radiation leakages including Sector B. Sector A however has been reported to have the least amount of radiation although still unsafe at this time. **The facility will be closed off for immediate deep cleaning. The public has nothing to worry about as none of the radiation has been reported to be leaking out of the facility.**

---

**Context:** Public news report issued 5 days after the 1987 Thermal Runaway. The report confirms the 4-cell ejection, the evacuation, and the facility closure. The bolded public reassurance is notable given the severity of the contamination. Related pages: [[S F R Mark I|S-F-R-Mark-I]], [[Meridian Depthworks Centre|Meridian-Depthworks-Centre]].
