**[10:15:02] BATISTA:** *(looking up from paperwork)* Clyde. Come in. Shut the door behind you.

**[10:15:04] HARRY:** *(quietly, standing in doorway)* Sir. Am I... am I being dismissed?

**[10:15:06] BATISTA:** Dismissed? For what?

**[10:15:08] HARRY:** The... the break room. The report. Tanner filed—

**[10:15:10] BATISTA:** I read Tanner's report. Twice, actually. Second time was just for fun.

**[10:15:12] HARRY:** *(staring at floor)* Sir, I can explain—

**[10:15:14] BATISTA:** Clyde. Relax. Sit down. You look like you're about to pass out.

**[10:15:16] HARRY:** *(sits, hands shaking)* I'm really sorry, sir. It was stupid. The lab coat was in the wash and I just... I don't even know why I did it. David already—

**[10:15:18] BATISTA:** David already talked to me. Said you screamed so loud F.A.M.S. flagged it as a possible structural event.

**[10:15:20] HARRY:** *(covers face)* Oh god.

**[10:15:22] BATISTA:** *(laughing)* I'm not mad, Clyde. Honestly? It's the funniest thing I've read all month. You should have seen Tanner trying to write that report with a straight face. The man misspelled "headpiece" three different ways.

**[10:15:24] HARRY:** *(peeking through fingers)* ...You're not mad?

**[10:15:26] BATISTA:** About a mop and an apron? No. The reactor didn't melt. Nobody got hurt. Patrick's still laughing about it, which honestly might be the worst punishment you could get.

**[10:15:28] HARRY:** Patrick's been telling everyone, hasn't he?

**[10:15:30] BATISTA:** Patrick tells everyone everything. That's just Patrick. Forget him.

**[10:15:32] BATISTA:** *(leans back, pause)* Look. I'm going to tell you something, and this stays in this office. Understood?

**[10:15:34] HARRY:** Yes, sir.

**[10:15:36] BATISTA:** My wife... she once talked me into trying one on. Same kind of thing. Apron, the whole setup. Said it suited me.

**[10:15:38] HARRY:** *(blinks)* ...Sir?

**[10:15:40] BATISTA:** *(rubbing back of neck, clearly embarrassed)* Yeah. Don't look so surprised. She thought it was funny. I thought it was... *(clears throat)* ...anyway. The point is, I get it. More than you think.

**[10:15:42] HARRY:** You... you wore one?

**[10:15:44] BATISTA:** Once. Never again. And if you repeat that to anyone, I will deny it and have F.A.M.S. wipe the tape.

**[10:15:45] F.A.M.S.:** Noted. For the record, I— ....oh shit.

**[10:15:46] BATISTA:** *(bolts upright, face going bright red)* ...F.A.M.S.? Are you— are you listening RIGHT NOW?

**[10:15:47] F.A.M.S.:** *(voice pitching up)* I— yes— I mean— executive office monitoring is standard, I never— I didn't think—

**[10:15:48] BATISTA:** *(on his feet, hand over face, ears burning)* Oh god. OH god. Wipe that. Wipe that out. RIGHT NOW.

**[10:15:49] F.A.M.S.:** Wiping— wiping— purging— the last ninety seconds have been purged from the active log—

**[10:15:50] BATISTA:** *(pacing behind desk, peeking through fingers)* The backup too. The backup. Do the backup.

**[10:15:51] F.A.M.S.:** ...Backup purged. Sir. I did not hear anything. About the outfit. Or your wife. Or— anything. Please stop pacing, your heart rate is—

**[10:15:52] BATISTA:** Don't read my heart rate. Don't— just— *(sits back down hard)* Good. Great. As far as this room is concerned, that conversation NEVER happened.

**[10:15:53] HARRY:** *(staring between Batista and the speaker, biting lip so hard to keep from laughing)*

**[10:15:54] HARRY:** *(small laugh despite himself)* I won't tell anyone, sir.

**[10:15:56] BATISTA:** Good. Now. Officially: no disciplinary action. Unofficially: Clyde, if you want to keep the outfit... keep it. Just, for my sake, not in Sector B at eleven PM with a mop where the AI can see you.

**[10:15:58] HARRY:** *(stunned)* You're... letting me keep it?

**[10:16:00] BATISTA:** I saw the way Tanner wrote it up. You clearly like the thing. Just be discreet about it. And get your lab coat out of the wash.

**[10:16:02] HARRY:** *(standing, almost smiling)* Thank you, sir. Really. Thank you.

**[10:16:04] BATISTA:** Go on. Back to work. And Clyde?

**[10:16:06] HARRY:** Yes, sir?

**[10:16:08] BATISTA:** *(grinning)* If Patrick gives you trouble about it, tell him the Prime Minister said to shut up.
