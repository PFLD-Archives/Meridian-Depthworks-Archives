**[14:14:04] CONTROL (DELGADO):** *(radio)* Control room copies DVP failure. Board confirms it. R:O team, you're the only souls left in Sector B. Evacuation's complete everywhere else.

**[14:14:06] KOVAC:** *(radio, distant, out of breath)* —from the far station, I threw the DVP switch, it's thrown, nothing's happening down here—

**[14:14:10] BECKETT:** *(slamming the panel)* Ejector terminal's dead anyway. Hydraulics aren't responding. The controls are locked out and the manual release is jammed. We can't fire from out here.

**[14:14:13] MOREAU:** Thermal index nine point four and climbing. Two minutes to ten. Then it doesn't matter what we do.

**[14:14:15] KOVAC:** *(radio, running footsteps)* I'm coming, I'm coming— tell me there's another way to drop the cells, right? Right?

**[14:14:17] [INTERCOM] F.A.M.S.:** *(static, then clearer than it has been all day)* ...Attention, any remaining personnel. Standard protocols have been exhausted. One option remains.

**[14:14:20] [INTERCOM] F.A.M.S.:** Enter the internal structure. Reach the ejection bolts. Release all four by hand, together, within a one to three second synchronization. I cannot promise this will work. I cannot promise you will walk out. But it is the only chance left.

**[14:14:24] BECKETT:** Inside? The chamber's flooding with— we'd be standing next to four live cells at Shatter Point—

**[14:14:26] [INTERCOM] F.A.M.S.:** Trust me... go.

**[14:14:28] MOREAU:** *(quietly)* ...Golden glow on the viewport. Fission luminescence. We're out of time to argue.

**[14:14:29] KOVAC:** *(bursting through the door, gasping)* ...made it. I'm here.

**[14:14:30] OKAFOR:** Then we go inside. All four of us. Each of us takes a bolt. Kovac?

**[14:14:32] KOVAC:** *(breathing hard)* ...Yeah. Yeah, I'm in.

**[14:14:34] <CLANGING. BOOTS ON METAL. RADIATION ALARMS RISING.>**

**[14:14:41] OKAFOR:** *(shouting over the roar)* Positions! Hands on your bolt! On my count! Three... two... one... PULL.

**[14:14:43] *FOUR BOLTS RELEASE. THE CELLS COME FREE IN THEIR HANDS.***

**[14:14:45] MOREAU:** *(screaming over the noise)* We've got them! They're out but we're still holding them!

**[14:14:46] OKAFOR:** Then run! Out of the structure! Throw them down the pit!

**[14:14:48] <BOOTS POUNDING ON METAL. FOUR CELLS ARC DOWN INTO THE BORATED PIT.>**

**[14:14:50] BECKETT:** All four away! Cells are in the pit!

**[14:14:52] KOVAC:** STI— nine point one... eight point four... it's falling! It's actually falling!

**[14:14:54] *A DEEP SHOCKWAVE ROLLS OUT FROM THE CHAMBER AS COMBUSTION DIES. THE REMAINING C-2500 UNITS WHINE DOWN AND GO DARK ONE BY ONE.***

**[14:14:56] OKAFOR:** *(gasping)* Utility lift! Everybody in! We're going down, away from this thing!

**[14:14:58] MOREAU:** What about the other lift?

**[14:15:00] OKAFOR:** That one goes up toward the reactor. Medics take that one to reach us. We take this one down and out. MOVE.

**[14:14:55] [INTERCOM] F.A.M.S.:** *(fragmenting)* —firm— ejection confirmed... combustion stalled... medical teams to Sector B... repeat, medical—

**[14:14:57] MOREAU:** F.A.M.S.? F.A.M.S., say again!

**[14:14:59] [INTERCOM] F.A.M.S.:** *(voice breaking apart)* S- System Error. Malfunction detecte— *(dissolving into grinding static)*

**[14:15:01] *A LOUD CRASH RIPS THROUGH THE INTERCOM. DEAD STATIC. THEN SILENCE. EVERY STATUS LIGHT ON THE WALL GOES DARK.***

**[14:15:03] BECKETT:** F.A.M.S.? F.A.M.S., come back. *(beat, quieter)* ...He's gone. The intercom just died.

**[14:15:05] MOREAU:** Look at the viewport. The glow's dimming. And listen— the whine's dying down. It worked. It actually worked.

**[14:15:07] KOVAC:** *(quietly, sliding down the lift wall)* I don't feel good.

**[14:15:09] MOREAU:** Stay down. Everybody just stay down, okay? Beckett, you look grey. You sit too.

**[14:15:11] OKAFOR:** *(into radio, voice thinning)* Control... four away... by hand... reactor stalled... we're in the utility lift heading down... we need medics... all four of us... *(pause)* ...tell them it worked.

**[14:15:13] CONTROL (DELGADO):** *(radio, breaking up)* ...Control copies four away. Say again your— *(static)* —we read you faint. Medics are already moving. Hold on.

**[14:15:15] SECURITY (OSEI):** *(radio)* Sector A post to control. Medical teams through the vault door now. We're taking the up lift toward the reactor. Everybody stay where you are.
