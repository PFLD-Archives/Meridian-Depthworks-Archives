**[14:14:14] KOVAC:** So what do we— there's no other way to drop the cells, right? Right?

**[14:14:17] MOREAU:** *(watching the board)* ...It's at nine point eight. It's not slowing down.

**[14:14:20] BECKETT:** Then we're out of time. We need to go. Now.

**[14:14:23] OKAFOR:** *(staring at the chamber)* ...Wait. Look at the viewport.

**[14:14:29] KOVAC:** What is that? What's that light?

**[14:14:34] [INTERCOM] F.A.M.S.:** *(automated alert tone)* Code Black. Repeat, Code Black. The S.F.R has entered full Thermal Runaway. All remaining personnel evacuate immediately. This is the final directive.

**[14:14:37] OKAFOR:** *(into radio)* Control, we copy Code Black. We're still in Sector B. We're not leaving.

**[14:14:40] CONTROL (DELGADO):** *(radio, breaking up)* ...Okafor, you can't— the whole sector's going critical—

**[14:14:43] OKAFOR:** Then it goes critical with four live cells still in it. We're not leaving them.

**[14:14:46] [INTERCOM] F.A.M.S.:** *(static, then clearer than it has been all day)* ...Attention, any remaining personnel. Standard protocols have been exhausted. One option remains.

**[14:14:49] [INTERCOM] F.A.M.S.:** Enter the internal structure. Reach the ejection bolts. Release all four by hand, together, within a one to three second synchronization. I cannot promise this will work. I cannot promise you will walk out. But it is the only chance left.

**[14:14:52] BECKETT:** Inside? The chamber's flooding with— we'd be standing next to four live cells at Shatter Point—

**[14:14:54] [INTERCOM] F.A.M.S.:** Trust me... go.

**[14:14:56] MOREAU:** *(quietly)* ...Golden glow on the viewport. Fission luminescence. We're out of time to argue.

**[14:14:58] OKAFOR:** Then we go inside. All four of us. Each of us takes a bolt. Kovac?

**[14:15:00] KOVAC:** *(breathing hard)* ...Yeah. Yeah, I'm in.

**[14:15:09] OKAFOR:** *(shouting over the roar)* Positions! Hands on your bolt! On my count! Three... two... one... PULL.

**[14:15:11] <FOUR BOLTS RELEASE. THE CELLS COME FREE IN THEIR HANDS.>**

**[14:15:13] MOREAU:** *(screaming over the noise)* We've got them! They're out but we're still holding them!

**[14:15:14] OKAFOR:** Then run! Out of the structure! Throw them down the pit!

**[14:15:18] BECKETT:** All four away! Cells are in the pit!

**[14:15:20] KOVAC:** STI— nine point one... eight point four... it's falling! It's actually falling!

**[14:15:24] OKAFOR:** *(gasping)* Utility lift! Everybody in! We're going down, away from this thing!

**[14:15:41] OKAFOR:** *(into radio, voice thinning)* Control... four away... by hand... reactor stalled... we're in the utility lift heading down... we need medics... all four of us... *(pause)* ...tell them it worked.
